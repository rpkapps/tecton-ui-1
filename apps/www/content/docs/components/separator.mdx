## RTL

Wrap your application in the [DirectionProvider](/docs/components/direction) and the component follows the reading direction — there is nothing else to configure.

<ComponentPreview name="separator-rtl" direction="rtl" />

## Emphasis

Use the `emphasis` prop for the three Tecton divider levels: `subtle`, `default` and `strong`. It works for both orientations.

<ComponentPreview name="separator-emphasis" />

## With label

A labelled divider is a `role="separator"` flex row with a `Separator` on each side of the text.
