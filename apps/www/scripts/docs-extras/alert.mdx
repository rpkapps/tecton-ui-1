## Tecton variants

The Tecton style adds the design system's status colours and surfaces to `Alert`: `variant` is the severity and `appearance` the surface. Icon, title, description and actions compose as in the examples above.

### Severity

Use `variant="success" | "warning" | "info" | "destructive"` and pass a lucide icon as the first child.

<ComponentPreview name="alert-severities" />

### Appearance

The default appearance keeps the card background and colours the text with the status colour (`--success`, `--warning`, …). `appearance="outline"` is transparent with a coloured border and text; `appearance="filled"` uses the filled status surface (`--success-surface`, … with its `-foreground` text).
